Layout questions should not wait until the inspection.

Commercial enquiries want to understand usable zones, office-to-warehouse relationships, access, amenities, courtyard or hardstand and how the tenancy actually flows. A measured plan lets the campaign answer those questions visually rather than burying them in listing copy.

Direct answers

Commercial floor-plan questions.

What types of commercial property can be planned?

Warehouses, showrooms, offices, retail premises, mixed office-and-warehouse tenancies and development property can be scoped depending on access and campaign requirements.

Can floor plans be booked with photography?

Yes. Photography, video, aerial, floor plans and site mapping can be combined into one commercial media brief.

Are marketing plans a substitute for survey drawings?

No. Marketing floor plans are prepared for property presentation and enquiry context unless a separate professional or statutory scope is expressly agreed. Legal, architectural, survey or certification requirements should use the appropriate qualified documentation.
