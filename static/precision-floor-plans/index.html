Commercial floor plans that let an enquiry read the tenancy before inspection.

Sixth Vision Commercial produces clear, measured floor plans for warehouses, showrooms, offices and development property campaigns. Plans are designed to sit alongside photography, film and aerial media so the marketing package explains both how the property looks and how it works.

Request a floor-plan brief

Why the plan matters

A buyer sees space. A commercial enquiry needs layout.

Commercial property decisions depend on circulation, office-to-warehouse balance, access, amenities, yard or courtyard relationships and the way rooms or tenancies connect. A clean floor plan makes those relationships legible before the first inspection.

For marketing use, Sixth Vision can pair the plan with photography, video, CASA-certified aerial and measured site mapping so the campaign carries a consistent visual language.
