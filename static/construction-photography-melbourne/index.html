What gets documented

Progress for the record. Finished work for the portfolio.

A construction brief can serve more than one audience. Project teams need clear visual records. Directors and clients need concise updates. Marketing teams need polished assets that communicate scale and finish. Sixth Vision can scope stills, video, vertical reels and aerial together so one site visit produces useful material across those jobs.

For longer projects, milestone or recurring visits can be planned around the build programme. The scope is agreed to the project rather than forcing every site into the same template.

Aerial & site context

When the ground view cannot explain the scale.

CASA-certified aerial photography and video can show site access, footprint, surrounding roads, neighbouring development and construction progress from a viewpoint that ground photography cannot. Where a property campaign later needs it, Sixth Vision can also combine aerial frames with boundary, frontage and amenity mapping.

Useful beyond marketing

One visual record can serve several teams.

Construction imagery can support progress updates, stakeholder presentations, tender submissions, capability decks, recruitment, social content and handover communications. Where formal evidentiary or contractual documentation is required, the photography brief should be agreed with the project team; Sixth Vision media does not replace survey, engineering or legal records.

Direct answers

Construction photography questions.

Do you photograph live construction sites?

Yes, subject to site access, induction and safety requirements agreed with the project team. The brief can cover ground photography, video and CASA-certified aerial where conditions and approvals allow.

Can you return at project milestones?

Yes. Recurring or milestone coverage can be scoped around the programme so comparable stages are captured intentionally rather than as unrelated one-off visits.

Can the same shoot produce social content?

Yes. Photography, project film and vertical reels can be planned together so the site visit produces both formal project assets and social-ready edits.

Do you cover completed commercial builds too?

Yes. Completed industrial, office, retail and development projects can be photographed for marketing, leasing, capability statements and builder portfolios.
