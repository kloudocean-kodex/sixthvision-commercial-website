Delivered work · Cremorne, Melbourne

A commercial floor plan that reads like a leasing brief.

At 7 Kipling Mews, Cremorne, the plan needed to do more than show rooms. It makes the relationship between showroom, warehouse, offices, amenities, roller-door access and the 17.4 metre courtyard immediately legible.

The commercial problem

A tenancy can be attractive and still be hard to understand online.

Commercial enquiries often need to know how the operational spaces connect before they inspect: where the office component sits, how the warehouse is accessed, whether there is usable courtyard space, and how the tenancy flows.

A dimensioned plan reduces that uncertainty and gives the listing team a reusable reference for portals, brochures and enquiry follow-up.

What the deliverable does

One asset, built to answer the next question.

01 · Showroom + warehouse

The plan separates customer-facing and operational space so the tenancy reads instantly.

02 · Offices + amenities

Four offices and service areas are drawn into the same measured context rather than described separately.

03 · Courtyard + RSD

The 17.4 metre courtyard and roller-door access remain visible as functional leasing information.

Why this matters

The plan becomes a working sales document.

For agents and occupiers, a measured plan can answer layout questions before an inspection and reduce the friction of explaining the tenancy repeatedly. It also gives photography and video a factual layer rather than asking visuals to do every job.
