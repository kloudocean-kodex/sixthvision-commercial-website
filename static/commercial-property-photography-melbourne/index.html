Direct answers

Commercial property photography questions.

Short answers to the questions agencies, asset managers and property teams ask before a commercial shoot.

What does commercial property photography include?

A typical brief covers the building exterior, interiors, key operational areas, access, frontage and detail shots. For industrial or land campaigns, aerial context, floor plans and site or boundary mapping can be added to explain the asset more completely.

Do you photograph warehouses and industrial property?

Yes. Sixth Vision Commercial photographs warehouses, logistics facilities, factories, hardstand, showrooms and industrial estates across Melbourne and Victoria, with attention to scale, loading, clearance and access.

Can drone and aerial imagery be included?

Yes. CASA-certified aerial photography and video can show site context, access, surrounding infrastructure and land scale. Boundary, frontage and amenity overlays can be added where the campaign needs them.

How quickly are commercial images delivered?

24-hour delivery is available for commercial photography. Larger briefs involving video, measurement or complex mapping are scoped around the campaign deadline.

Do you service only Melbourne CBD?

No. Sixth Vision Commercial works across metropolitan Melbourne and Victoria for commercial, industrial, office, retail, land and development property campaigns.

Can one shoot cover photography, video, plans and mapping?

Yes. The media brief can combine stills, film, aerial, measured floor plans and site or boundary mapping so the agency receives one coherent campaign package.
