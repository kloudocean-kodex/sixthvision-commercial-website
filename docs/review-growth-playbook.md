# Sixth Vision Commercial — Review Growth Playbook

Last reviewed: 2026-09-21

Purpose: increase genuine Google review recency and depth without incentives, review gating, keyword scripts or fabricated feedback.

## Canonical Google review destination

- Managed Commercial GBP Place ID: `ChIJ2Z_9wQ9N1moR5ODTc2YpIfk`
- Direct review URL: https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=ChIJ2Z_9wQ9N1moR5ODTc2YpIfk

## Trigger

Ask after a clearly successful delivery, preferably after the client has acknowledged the files or used the campaign. Do not ask only customers predicted to leave five stars. Do not offer discounts, gifts or other consideration for reviews.

## One-touch message

> Thanks again for trusting Sixth Vision Commercial with the property. If the photography, video, aerial or measured media helped the campaign, would you mind leaving an honest Google review? It helps other Melbourne property teams understand what working with us is like: https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=ChIJ2Z_9wQ9N1moR5ODTc2YpIfk

## Follow-up

At most one polite reminder if there is no response. Do not chase repeatedly.

## What not to script

Do not tell customers which keywords, suburb, service or star rating to mention. Natural language is more credible and avoids manipulative review patterns.

## GrowthProof fields

For each request, if/when a delivery system is connected, record:
- project/job reference
- request date
- channel
- delivery-success signal
- whether a reminder was sent
- review URL/id if a public review later appears
- no personal message contents in GA4

## Baseline

At the 2026-09-21 verification pass the canonical Commercial GBP had 10 reviews and a 5.0 average. This is a baseline, not a target or guarantee. Track review recency, new genuine reviews and qualified GBP actions rather than chasing an arbitrary count.
